Software engineering case study

Mission Guatemala

Healthcare, pharmacy, and field-operations software designed around the real workflows of a nonprofit medical clinic and its teams in Guatemala.

Role
Full-Stack Software Engineer & Subject Matter Expert
Technologies
C#, .NET, WinForms, MySQL, Swagger, PWA
Period
February 2024–June 2026

The work

I developed and maintained software supporting clinical workflow, patient processing, reporting, and database operations for Mission Guatemala’s medical clinic. The work required translating real clinical and administrative processes into software that staff could use reliably.

I served as the team’s subject matter expert, converting stakeholder needs into technical designs, reviewing development work, and supporting the system after launch.

Pharmacy inventory and prescriptions

I designed and built a pharmacy inventory and prescription management system from ambiguous requirements. The system supports a clinic dispensing more than 10,000 medications annually.

  • Modeled pharmacy inventory and prescription workflows.
  • Built the application with C#, .NET, WinForms, and MySQL.
  • Led a team of six engineers through design and implementation.
  • Defined technical direction, reviewed work, and tracked project milestones.
Mission Guatemala published an independent account of the electronic database launch, providing public context for the project and its impact.
